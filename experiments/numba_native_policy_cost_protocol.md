**Experimental observation only. NO production candidate; do NOT merge.** Original `master` pinned at `4c1c410d8eb04547421f72ee1d3ca2eeb5763ff2`, which includes #228. This protocol is committed **before** any diagnostic code or measurements.

## Why a new profile

[#208](https://github.com/fbreve/pypcc/pull/208) attributed ≈93–98% of long original native `fit_predict` to propagation, while [#219](https://github.com/fbreve/pypcc/pull/219) measured real native Callgrind instruction counts and warned not to equate instruction counts to CPU time. The original Numba step has an actual `np.random.random()<p_grd` greedy/random decision, with extra cumulative-weight work in the greedy path, and a separate C/F dominance/degree layout. Multiple earlier source-changing fused-greedy, direct-indexing, RNG and rowmax proposals already received NO-GO (#107, #114, #213, #214, #220, #221, #229, #230). This study **does not repeat or modify any of those candidates**.

Measure the original, completely unchanged actual Numba kernel in genuine complete fits under frozen distinct movement policies `p_grd = 0.0, 0.5, 1.0`, with frozen kNN neighborhood geometries. The purpose is to quantify *policy-conditional* native and non-native cost and detect if a future genuinely different hypothesis is worthwhile. **Changing p_grd changes trajectories, particle locations, distance table evolution and possibly visited-node ratios**; the resulting CPU difference is NOT a causal pure cost of one greedy branch, and no unsupported percentage of CPU shall be attributed to specific instructions. We do not compare states across different `p_grd` conditions.
