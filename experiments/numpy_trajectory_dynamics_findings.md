## What the results actually show

1. **Endpoint-quiet `!=` continuous label stability.** Among **185** globally endpoint-quiet complete windows, **13** (about 7%) contained at least one unlabeled node whose class changed temporarily and then reverted by the window endpoint. The aggregate across all windows was **118 reverted node-window events**, not 118 distinct dataset nodes. A reversed node can occur even in a window where some other node changed permanently; thus not every reversed event happens in a globally endpoint-quiet window. Do not call sparse checkpoint net differences an exhaustive flip count.
2. **Aggressive potential stopping can differ from continued classifications.** Six of eight seeds stopped by 6001 (Iris/Wine/Breast Cancer), and **five of those six** had a different final predicted label on at least one initially unlabeled node after uninterrupted continuation. This is not observer interference: the exact same RNG and native state are established at the true stopping iteration, and no-stop observed/unobserved parity is exact.
3. **Continued accuracy was never lower in this tiny selected pilot.** Five of the early-stopping seeds had higher held-out unlabeled accuracy by iteration 6001, and one stayed equal. For the two Digits seeds, no potential stop occurred and no comparison difference exists. This is **not** evidence that disabling early stopping universally improves accuracy, nor does it evaluate the ordinary `es_chk=2000` default or establish statistical significance. Extra computation trades off against any possible later gains, and trajectories are not necessarily monotonic.
4. **No permanently immobile particle in these runs.** Across the eight 6001-step observed trajectories, all **72** particles changed node at least once. The longest consecutively unchanged-position streak was only **11 iterations** across the entire pilot (Iris 5/5, Wine 8/8, Breast Cancer 7/5, Digits 11/8, for seeds 18013/18109); no iteration had zero particles changing position. This is specific to four dense reciprocal union-10NN graphs with seeded labels and does **not** rule out longer streaks or traps in sparse/disconnected or differently parameterized graphs. Position-only observations cannot distinguish rejected movement attempts from other causes of immobility. The complete per-particle streak distributions are in the archived raw JSON.
5. **No causal convergence claim from potential or short windows.** The original potential criterion measures mean maximum dominance at a fixed check cadence, not monotonic classification stability. We tested one aggressive patience setting and only eight exploratory trajectories. A robust decision about an alternative stopping policy requires independent datasets, more seeds, suitable compute-versus-accuracy evaluation and preregistered criteria. No policy change follows from this experiment.

## Engineering decision

Preserve **#183 as draft**, targeting experimental #182, which is also draft/unmerged. Keep all production defaults untouched. The next research step, if justified, is a separate preregistered broader replication evaluating (a) length/distribution of stationary streaks, (b) the default potential stopping behavior and (c) accuracy-vs-computation tradeoff with multiple independent seeds. Do **not** add force-minimum movement or particle reset merely because a particle's position remained unchanged.
